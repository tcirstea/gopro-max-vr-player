Quest VR controls
Either stick forward/back: zoom in/out. Right/left: seek ±5 seconds across videos in the sorted playlist (hold to repeat; stops at playlist ends). A / X: play/pause. Click either stick: reset zoom only (keep view). Hold either grip + move/rotate controller: drag/rotate view. Either index trigger / bumper above the grip: reveal controls where that controller points. Release, then press again to select. Press again while aiming away from the panel to hide it. B / Y also toggles controls. Aim either controller and use its trigger to select or drag the progress bar, then release to seek. Playlist opens on the current video’s page, with list/grid views that retain your place, paging, and sorting that changes playback order. Selecting a video hides all controls and starts it. Mute/Unmute shares the browser audio setting; audio-style selection remains in the browser. The current filename and embedded file date appear as text above the panel without enlarging it. Dates retain the file’s stored clock, with timezone unverified; missing dates are marked unavailable. Controls hide after two seconds of playing, but stay while paused, buffering, interacting, or browsing the playlist.
