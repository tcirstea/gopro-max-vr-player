Experimental: native MAX + VR
Dual-track HEVC decoding, MAX EAC projection and immersive WebXR are implemented but not yet verified in a real browser/headset. Synchronized native stereo and metadata-verified spatial headphone audio are experimental; use headphones for head-relative listening. Requires compatible HEVC hardware and browser support.
Quest VR controls
Right stick forward/back: zoom in/out. Right/left: seek ±5 seconds (hold to repeat). A: play/pause. Click right stick: recenter + reset zoom. Hold right grip + move/rotate controller: drag/rotate view. Trigger + stick turning is not assigned yet.
